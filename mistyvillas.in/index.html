Experiences & Comforts

Beautiful Hilltop Location

Located on top of the Panchalimedu Hills, Misty Villas is surrounded by nature. With average temperatures of 15°C - you can enjoy the amazing mountain view right from your room.

BBQ, Campfire, & More

Enjoy delicious BBQ, campfire and hearty Veg & Non-Veg meals for Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner. Take a refreshing dip in our swimming pool surrounded by cold climate of the hills

Nearby Scenic Spots

Our resort is very close to Parunthumpara viewpoint, Valanjanganam Waterfalls Vagamon Pine Forest, Sita Devi Lake & more
