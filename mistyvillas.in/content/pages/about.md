Misty Villas is a small, family-run resort on top of the Panchalimedu hills in Kerala. With average temperatures of around 15°C, you can enjoy the mountain view right from your room.

## What we offer

- Spacious rooms with balconies and hill views
- Veg and non-veg meals for breakfast, lunch and dinner
- BBQ and campfire
- A swimming pool surrounded by the cool hill climate

## Find us

We are close to Parunthumpara viewpoint, Valanjanganam Waterfalls, Vagamon Pine Forest and Sita Devi Lake. Call or WhatsApp us to plan your stay.
