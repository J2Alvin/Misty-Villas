  - name: Peeru Hills
    text: |-
      A renowned hill station offering a relaxed vibe for trekkers. Named after a Sufi saint, it's a popular spot for its panoramic views and rustic trails.

      Visitors can also explore nearby sites like the saint's mausoleum and a royal summer palace.
    photos:
      - image: /images/nearby2.webp
        alt: Misty hill road near Panchalimedu
  - name: Paruthumpara
    text: |-
      Parunthumpara is a hidden gem offering an unparalleled panoramic view of lush green forests. From its vantage point, you can see the Sabarimala forests stretching as far as the eye can see.

      This tranquil spot is a must-visit for anyone seeking natural beauty.
    photos:
      - image: /images/parunthumpara.webp

  - name: Ammachi Kottaram
    text: |-
      Constructed in the 19th century by Travancore Maharaja Moolam Thirunal, Ammachi Kottaram served as a royal retreat for his wife, the consort. Situated in the cool, tea-growing highlands, its exact history remains unclear.

      The palace has recently been a filming location for Malayalam films such as Carbon (2018) and Lucifer (2019).
    photos:
      - image: /images/pine_forest_2.webp
        alt: Forest stream in the Idukki highlands
  - name: Valanjam Waterfalls
    text: |-
      Visible only from June to September, this picture-perfect waterfall tumbles off a cliff as if fired from a cannon, framed by canopies of trees.

      You can even pull over at the roadside to enjoy a hot drink with the gushing water in the background.
